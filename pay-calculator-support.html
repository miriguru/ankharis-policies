My take-home doesn't match my payslip

Open Your details (the year and region at the top of Pay) and check your pension and how it's paid (salary sacrifice, net pay or relief at source), your student loan plan, and your tax code. If your payslip shows a tax code other than the one the app works out, enter it there. Payslips can also differ month to month because of bonuses, RSU vests or a tax code change partway through the year.

Why did my take-home go down after updating to 4.3?

Version 4.3 corrected income tax for salaries over £100,000. Earlier versions didn't move the higher-rate threshold down as the tax-free allowance tapered away, so they showed too little tax (up to about £2,500 a year). The new figures follow HMRC's rules.

How are RSUs taxed in the app?

Each vest is added to your pay for the UK tax year it lands in (6 April to 5 April), valued in pounds at the vest-date share price and exchange rate, and taxed with your salary. The sell-to-cover figures are estimates: your employer withholds at its own rates, so check your payslip.

My vesting schedule doesn't match my broker

Enter the schedule from your first vest date, then edit any vest's date or share count before saving. After saving, tap a vest on the RSUs tab and use Edit this vest.

Share prices aren't updating

Prices and exchange rates need an internet connection. Pull down on the RSUs tab to refresh. If a price can't be fetched, the last known value is kept.

Where is my data stored?

On your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage so it syncs to your other devices. See the privacy policy.
