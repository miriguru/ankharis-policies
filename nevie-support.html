What do Safe, Limit and Avoid mean?

Safe foods are generally fine to eat during pregnancy. Limit foods are fine in moderation, with a note on how much or how often. Avoid foods carry a known risk, such as listeria or high mercury. Verdicts are general guidance based on public food-safety advice and, for foods outside the built-in library, AI-generated answers. They are not medical advice. Always check with your midwife or doctor about your own situation.

A food is missing or a verdict looks wrong

The built-in library covers 400+ foods and works offline. Anything else is answered by AI (Premium). If a verdict looks wrong, email us the food name and the language you were using and we will review it.

The scanner says I have no free scans left

Barcode and ingredients-label scanning includes 3 free scans. After that, scanning is part of Premium. Barcodes are looked up on Open Food Facts, a public product database, so some products, especially own-brand or very new ones, may not be found. Photographing the ingredients list works for any product.

I bought Premium but it is not unlocked

Open the Premium screen (tap any Premium feature, or Settings → Free vs Premium) and tap Restore Purchases. If it still does not unlock, check you are signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase, then email us.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: go to iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions and choose Nevie. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged.

Where is my history and due date stored?

Only on your device. Your due date, dietary options, search history and free-scan count never leave your iPhone and are removed if you delete the app. Nevie has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or location.

Changing the language

Nevie is available in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese. Open Settings → Language in the app to switch.
