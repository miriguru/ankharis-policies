Stitchling — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Stitchling is made by Ankharis Ltd (United Kingdom).

Stitchling does not collect any personal data. There are no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs. The app has no servers of its own and never sends your information anywhere.

What the app stores, and where

Everything Stitchling saves is game data: your puzzle progress, finished knits, Buttons balance, dyes, which critter is wearing what, your streak, and your settings.

This is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud Drive enabled, it is also synced through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) into your own private iCloud database, so your progress survives reinstalling the app or moving to a new device.

That private database belongs to you. Ankharis Ltd cannot see it, cannot query it, and has no access to its contents. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Stitchling offers optional one-time purchases (Premium Skein and Button packs). These are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Ankharis Ltd never receives or stores your payment details, card number, or billing address. The app only learns whether a purchase completed, so it can unlock what you paid for.

Advertising

There is no advertising in Stitchling. There are no forced ads, no interstitials, no rewarded video, and no ad networks embedded in the app. This is a deliberate product decision, not a setting.

Tracking

Stitchling does not track you across apps or websites. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and will never show you the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Children

Stitchling is suitable for all ages and is rated 4+. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete. To remove all Stitchling data, delete the app from your device and, if you use iCloud sync, remove its data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
