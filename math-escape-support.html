How do I play?

Answer each sum before Dash runs out of path. Every right answer adds a step; a wrong answer, or taking too long, costs one. Get 5 in a row for double points and 10 in a row for triple. Tap How to Play on the main menu for the full rules.

What do the difficulty levels change?

Easy asks addition and subtraction, Medium adds multiplication, and Hard uses all four operations. Harder levels run faster and score more points. Pick one right above Start Playing.

Can I practise without the timer?

Yes. Practice has no clock and no falling, and lets you choose which operations to work on, so you can drill just times tables if you like.

My scores did not carry over to a new device

Turn on Use iCloud in Settings on both devices, and make sure both are signed in to the same Apple Account. High scores, achievements and stats then stay the same on each.

Game Center scores are not showing

Check you are signed in to Game Center in the Settings app. Scores and achievements are reported when a run ends.

I left a tip but nothing changed

Tips are a thank-you and do not unlock anything; the whole game is free. If you were charged twice or something looks wrong, email us.

Are there really no ads?

Really. Math Escape has no advertising and no tracking.
